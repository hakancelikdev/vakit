Ad Policy

Last updated: 10 September 2026 — Version 1.7.4 In an Islamic app, the ads you see become part of your worship moment. That is why Vakit shows no ads. The one exception is opt-in: a user who wants to support the app can choose to watch a short rewarded video ad. This page describes the rules that apply to those ads.

1. Where Ads Appear

Vakit shows no banner, interstitial or app-open ads on any screen. The only ad is a short rewarded video that a user can choose to watch from the Support Center ("Keep Vakit Alive", "Sadaka Mode") to support the app. You never have to use it, and no feature depends on it.

2. Categories We Never Show

Ads in the following categories are blocked on the ad network: • Gambling, betting, casino, sports betting, poker • Alcohol and tobacco products • Adult, dating, sexual content, sex products • Nudity in any form • Interest-based loans, payday loans, interest-bearing investments • Fortune-telling, astrology, psychic readings, magic, tarot • Pork and pork products • Get-rich-quick schemes, MLM traps • Religious propaganda from other faiths

3. Technical Safeguards

These settings are applied on the ad network side (Google AdMob): • Maximum content rating: G (general audience). • In Brand Safety > Block Content, all categories above are flagged. • URL and keyword denylist: ads containing terms like "casino", "betting", "gambling", "flirt", "dating", "horoscope", "psychic", "tarot" cannot pass. • Ads are always non-personalized; Vakit never shows the App Tracking Transparency prompt and the advertising identifier (IDFA) is not shared.

4. If You See an Inappropriate Ad

If you see an ad that conflicts with your religious sensitivities, write to hakancelikdev@gmail.com with a screenshot. Every valid report is added to the AdMob denylist.

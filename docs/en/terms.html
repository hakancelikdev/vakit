2. Description of Service

Vakit is a free app providing worship tools such as prayer times, qibla direction, Quran, hadith, a dhikr counter, prayer/ablution guides, khatm and worship tracking, the Friday sermon, nearby mosques, and a religious-day calendar. It runs on iOS 26+ and macOS 26+, and ships a companion Apple Watch app for watchOS 26+.

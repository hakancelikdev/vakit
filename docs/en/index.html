§ 02 · Emanet — a trust

Your data is a trust,
not a product.

Don't let your worship be interrupted. Other prayer apps have been caught selling location data. Vakit went a different way: your coordinates never leave your phone. No account. No tracking. No compromise. This isn't a feature — it's a principle.

01 / no account

Download. Open. Pray.

No sign-up, no login, no password you'll forget. Vakit starts working the moment you install it.

02 / on-device

Calculated on your phone.

Prayer times are computed locally from your location, using the method you choose. Nothing is sent to a server to be 'looked up'.

03 / no tracking

GPS stays with you.

Your coordinates are not transmitted anywhere. We can't see where you pray, and we don't want to.

04 / transparent

Plain-language policy.

Usage counts that carry no identity. Our privacy policy is short enough to read in a minute — and it will stay that way.

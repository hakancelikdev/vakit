Emanet — a trust

Your data is a trust, not a product.

Other prayer apps have been caught selling location data. Vakit went a different way: your coordinates never reach our servers. No account. No ads. Your data is never sold. This isn't a feature — it's a principle.

Download. Open. Pray.

No sign-up, no login, no password you'll forget. Vakit starts working the moment you install it.

Calculated on your phone.

Prayer times are computed locally from your location, using the method you choose. Nothing is sent to a server to be 'looked up'.

Your exact location isn't ours.

Your coordinates never reach our servers — we only know your country, city and district. For your address and nearby mosques, location goes to Apple Maps only.

Plain-language policy.

Usage data tied to a pseudonymous code — no name, email or phone. The privacy policy lists exactly what is sent, and it's short enough to read in a minute.

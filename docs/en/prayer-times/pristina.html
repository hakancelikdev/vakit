Everything, nothing extra

A companion, not an attention machine.

20 core tools, each designed to feel calm the moment you open it.

  • Exactly as it should be.

    Prayer times by your chosen method; makruh and voluntary times are optional. Let the screen change with the sky, or keep it plain.

  • Without unlocking.

    The next prayer on your Lock Screen, in a widget, in the Dynamic Island and on your watch. A quiet glance — never a stare.

  • Every tool, in one place.

    Quran, hadith, Friday sermon, dhikr, asma, guides and the calendar — all in one hub. Nothing locked behind a paywall.

  • The right direction.

    Turn toward the qibla and your phone vibrates, with the distance to the Ka'bah alongside.

  • Your worship, in rings.

    Rings for prayer, Quran, dhikr, hadith and qada; how soon after each time enters you pray, and your khatm — gentle progress, no guilt.

  • Qada, cleared as you pray.

    Calculate a lifetime of missed prayers by time slot and clear them one by one — with an estimate of when you'll be done.

  • The Quran, even offline.

    Read, listen, reflect — Madinah or Türkiye script, word-by-word follow-along, tafsir and downloadable recitation.

  • Seventeen prayers, illustrated.

    From wudu to tasbih; standing, seated or by gesture — step by step, taught without condescension.

  • A calendar that remembers.

    Hijri and Gregorian together. Holy nights, Ramadan, the Eids and the eve before — with the month's worship summary below.

  • The count, in hand.

    A dhikr counter and the post-prayer tasbihat; presets, your own dhikr and a daily goal.

  • Voluntary Prayer Times

    Ishraq, Duha, Awwabin and the parts of the night; optional.

  • Mushaf Script

    Madinah or Türkiye script — page layout follows too. Optional Tajweed Colors mark ikhfa, idgham, qalqalah, madd and more in the verse list.

  • Asma al-Husna

    99 names of Allah, with transliteration and meaning; add any as dhikr.

  • Tasbihat

    33-33-33, or the full post-prayer sequence.

  • Worship Tracking

    Rings for prayer, Quran, dhikr, hadith and qada.

  • Hadith

    Over 36,000 ahadith across seven major collections, in Arabic with translations in 16 languages.

  • Unified Search

    One search, every tool — type 'Baqarah 255' to jump to the ayah.

  • Nearby Mosques

    Find masajid via Apple Maps, without your location reaching our servers; map, distance, directions.

  • Live Activity

    Dynamic Island and lock-screen countdowns.

  • Imsakiye

    A whole month of prayer times in one list; Ramadan day count and iftar.

Qibla direction

The Qibla from Pristina is 138° clockwise from north (south-east); the Kaaba is 2,930 km away. Vakit's Qibla compass vibrates when your phone faces the right way.

Emanet — a trust

Your data is a trust, not a product.

Other prayer apps have been caught selling location data. Vakit went a different way: your coordinates never reach our servers. No account. No ads. Your data is never sold. This isn't a feature — it's a principle.

Download. Open. Pray.

No sign-up, no login, no password you'll forget. Vakit starts working the moment you install it.

Calculated on your phone.

Prayer times are computed locally from your location, using the method you choose. Nothing is sent to a server to be 'looked up'.

Your exact location isn't ours.

Your coordinates never reach our servers — we only know your country, city and district. For your address and nearby mosques, location goes to Apple Maps only.

Plain-language policy.

Usage data tied to a pseudonymous code — no name, email or phone. The privacy policy lists exactly what is sent, and it's short enough to read in a minute.
